Cocode packages — live examples

Web builds of the example apps in coco-de/co-package, rebuilt on every push to main.

  • open_board

    Drawing and annotation widget — pens, marker, eraser, shapes, lasso and text tools, multiple pages, recording and replay, and handwriting on top of an EPUB reader.

  • open_epub

    EPUB 2 / EPUB 3 reader widget — reflowable and fixed layout, highlights with notes, and a sample library covering RTL, MathML, vertical writing, Media Overlays and CFI.
