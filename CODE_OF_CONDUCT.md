# Code of Conduct

We welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies
to issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

- Treat people with respect. Discuss the proposal or code and explain the impact
  of a change.
- Make room for questions and different perspectives. Accept constructive
  feedback and give others a chance to respond.
- Respect privacy. Share another person's private information only with their
  permission.
- Keep harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, and unwanted sexual
  attention out of project spaces.

For a conduct issue, contact a maintainer privately or use GitHub's reporting
tools. Include the relevant links and what happened. Maintainers may remove
harmful content, limit participation, or ask GitHub to intervene.

Use the [security policy](SECURITY.md) to report a vulnerability in the code.
